    Capture tips
    • Lighting: even, diffuse light (overcast outdoor light is ideal). Avoid harsh or changing shadows.
    • Surface: matte, textured surfaces work best. Shiny/clear objects: dust with scanning spray or dry shampoo first.
    • Coverage: record a slow, steady 20-40 second orbit around the object, sweeping from a bit above to a bit below eye level. Keep the object filling the frame and hold it still.
    • Scale: place a ruler or object of known size next to it if you'll need real-world dimensions.
